Tips

  • Arithmetic and percentages: write the formula first (profit = selling price − cost price), then put the numbers in.
  • Equations: check your answer by putting it back into the equation — it takes ten seconds.
  • Statistics: know mean, median, mode, range and quartiles cold; they are quick marks.
  • Scenario and mental maths: estimate first, so you can rule out options that are far off.
