Frequently asked questions

About the USAT, the question bank and how RAXUSATPrep works.

Is RAXUSATPrep free?

Yes. Practice, mocks, revision, essays and every other feature are free, with no sign-up and no ads.

Is this the official HEC or ETC test?

RAXUSATPrep is an independent practice platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by HEC or ETC. Always confirm the latest test pattern, dates and eligibility on official HEC/ETC sources.

Where do the questions come from?

From the TIPS Educational Consultants USAT books for Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Quantitative Reasoning and Verbal Reasoning. RAXUSATPrep checked every answer, corrected keys that were wrong, and wrote explanations where they help. Synonym and antonym questions are built from the Verbal Reasoning book’s word list.

What does “unverified” mean?

A few questions have options that the scan garbled (mostly maths symbols) or no correct printed option. They still appear in practice, labelled “Unverified” with the checked answer, but never in mocks or the daily challenge. Every question — with its checked answer and explanation — is also in the Question bank, written like the study PDFs.

Are all the questions from the books included?

Yes. Every question from the RAXUSATPrep Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Quantitative Reasoning and Verbal Reasoning question-and-answer documents is in the app, in book order, with the checked answer. Where the book’s key was wrong, the corrected answer is used and marked “Corrected key”.

What is the USAT pattern?

Part I has 75 MCQs in 100 minutes: 20 Verbal Reasoning, 25 Quantitative Reasoning and 30 subject questions for your category. Part II has two essays in 40 minutes — one argument essay (15 marks) and one narrative essay (10 marks), in English or Urdu. Always confirm the latest pattern on official HEC/ETC sources.

Which category should I choose?

Usually the one that matches your intermediate group: Pre-Medical → USAT-M, Pre-Engineering → USAT-E, ICS → USAT-CS, General Science → USAT-GS, FA/Humanities → USAT-A, I.Com → USAT-COM. Universities set their own requirements, so check the programme you are applying to.

Are there questions for Maths, Computer Science, Economics and the other subjects?

Not yet. The bank only uses questions from the source books we have, which cover Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Verbal and Quantitative Reasoning. For other categories you can practise Verbal and Quantitative Reasoning and the subjects we do have, and take a partial practice test that shows exactly what is missing.

Where is my progress saved?

In your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server. You can export a backup file from Settings and import it on another phone or computer.

Does it work on my phone and offline?

Yes. It is designed for phones first. Once the website is opened over the internet, it can be installed to your home screen and keeps working offline.

Is there negative marking?

RAXUSATPrep mocks use no negative marking by default. You can switch it on in Settings if you want to practise with it. Check the official notice for the current rule.

How are essays checked?

You check them yourself with a submission checklist and a 1–5 self-assessment for each marking point. RAXUSATPrep does not give AI scores. The templates, topic kits and model essays show what a strong essay looks like.

How do I report a mistake or contact you?

Use “Report” under any question in the app, or email rohilahmedsial@gmail.com. You can also reach us on LinkedIn or GitHub.
