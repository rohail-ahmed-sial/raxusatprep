How to prepare

Physics and Chemistry are in the bank. Mathematics (10 MCQs) is not yet — practise it from your FSc textbook while you use RAXUSATPrep for the rest.

  • Start with a quick set of 10 every day and keep your streak going.
  • Clear the revision queue first — it shows only questions due today.
  • Take one section test a week, then a full partial test every weekend.
  • Write one argument and one narrative essay a week using the templates.
