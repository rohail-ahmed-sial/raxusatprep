A simple weekly plan

  1. Every day: a quick 10, then clear your revision queue.
  2. Three days a week: 20 questions from your weakest topic (the dashboard lists them).
  3. Once a week: a section test, and one argument plus one narrative essay.
  4. Every weekend: a full mock (or partial test), then review every wrong answer.

Get the most from the app

  • Revision uses Leitner boxes: a wrong answer comes back the same day, then after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days.
  • Mistake journal: tag why you missed a question — concept, careless slip, misreading or time.
  • Flashcards: the full Verbal Reasoning word list with synonyms and antonyms.
  • Backup: Settings → Export backup. Import it on another device to continue.
  • Keyboard: 1–4 or A–D to answer, ← → to move, F to flag in tests.

Where the questions come from

The question bank is built from the TIPS Educational Consultants USAT books (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning) and their essay guide. RAXUSATPrep checked the answers, corrected wrong keys, and marks any question it could not confirm as unverified.
